## Open critical items (Review 1)
- **A15–A19 are implementation defects**, not design choices. The L3 layer does not function as a learning/compositional stage, and the L1 plastic-inhibition microcircuit is dead. The validated L2 competitive layer (8/8 unique symbols, maturity→1.0) is unaffected.
- **A8** (FEP branding) and **A1/A2** (global clock, zero delay) are the deepest *conceptual* exposures for a paper claiming biological computation.
